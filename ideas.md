## Selected Approach: **B — Sovereign Blueprint**

Deep navy authority with gold accents. Playfair Display headings, DM Sans body. Asymmetric layouts with diagonal dividers and large typographic numbers as visual anchors. This approach best communicates the institutional gravitas of a government trade platform while remaining modern and interactive.
