# Defect-Discovery Register

**Standard:** attached codebase-defect-discovery prompt
**Revision target:** current dirty worktree
**Method:** `CONFIRMED` findings have a read end-to-end executable path. `SUSPECTED` findings require a completed trace or live dependency evidence. No remediation decision is implied by discovery entries.

* The registration comments in the Go services remain `SUSPECTED` service-exposure defects until generated bindings, binary build, and runtime route registration are traced.
* No claim is made that the remaining F1–F16 families are clean. The service, money, trust-boundary, gate, and configuration map is recorded in `assurance/defect-discovery-ground-truth.md`; cross-check coverage is still incomplete.
